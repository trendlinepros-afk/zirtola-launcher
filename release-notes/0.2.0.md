## Zirtola Launcher 0.2.0

- **Updates every time you start.** The launcher checks for its own updates and
  for game updates when it opens, and again before you play if it's been a while.
  Games can update by themselves (Settings → Update games automatically).
- **Your saves are safe.** Updates, repairs and uninstalls only touch the game's
  own files. Uninstall asks whether to delete your saves too, and the answer
  starts at no.
- **One PC at a time, and it moves with you.** If your game is active on another
  PC, the launcher shows which one and moves it here in two clicks.
- **View license key** for each game you own.
- **What's new** for every game: release notes for each version, opened after an
  update.
- **System requirements** and **Report a bug** links on the game page.
- Games not on sale yet show their price with "Coming soon".
- Offline play keeps working for 7 days after the last licence check.
